How Lookout Works

Three connected roles. One seamless system.

1

Emergency Dispatched

Hospital admin receives a call and dispatches the nearest available ambulance with a single click.

2

Driver Navigates

The driver activates emergency mode. Lookout plots the fastest route and broadcasts alerts to nearby road users.

3

Road Clears

Road users within range receive an alert showing the ambulance direction and distance — and move aside.

4

Patient Reached

Ambulance reaches the patient faster. The driver then gets a direct route to the receiving hospital.
